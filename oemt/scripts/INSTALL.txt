OEmt.club - install guide
=========================

What you get
- OEmt.club inside Steam's main window: Sessions (your game history and your own public IP log),
  Settings and Diagnostics, right in Steam's top bar.
- The OEmt.club theme (dark blue-gray, indigo accents, Lucida Console) and a "Steam (original)" theme.
- A music player in Steam's sidebar for whatever is playing (Spotify, a browser tab, ...): cover art,
  back / play-pause / next, progress and system volume.
- Discord Rich Presence: your game and play time on your Discord profile, with a
  "Join OEmt.club" button (discord.gg/OEmt).

Requirements
- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) with the regular Steam client (stable branch).

Install
1. Fully close Steam: right-click the Steam icon in the taskbar tray, then Exit.
2. If Millennium is installed, remove it first: delete wsock32.dll and the "millennium" folder
   from your Steam folder (back them up if you want to go back). OEmt.club and Millennium can't be
   installed at the same time; both use wsock32.dll.
3. Extract the zip into your Steam folder (usually C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam). Allow it to
   replace files if asked. You should now have:
       Steam\wsock32.dll
       Steam\oemt\lib\       oemt.dll, oemt.hhx64.dll
       Steam\oemt\bin\       oemt.luavm64.exe, oemt.crashhandler64.exe, oemt-media.exe
       Steam\oemt\plugins\   club.oemt.core.star
       Steam\oemt\themes\    OEmt, SteamOriginal
4. Start Steam. OEmt.club turns itself on the first time.
5. Pick the theme: Steam menu > OEmt.club > Themes > OEmt.club > Use. Steam reloads its UI.

Discord Rich Presence
- Built in and on by default: no setup. With the Discord app running on the same PC, your profile
  shows the game you play, for how long, and a "Join OEmt.club" button.
- Settings > Discord Rich Presence: turn it off, show "In Steam" when idle, hide the button, or press
  Test ("Rich Presence is working" shows for 15 seconds). No login or token is used.

Good to know
- Everything runs on your PC. OEmt.club never asks for or stores passwords.
- The IP log is off until you turn it on (Sessions > My public IP) and only logs your own IP.
- The sidebar player can be turned off in Settings ("Music player in Steam's sidebar").
- If something looks wrong, open OEmt.club > Diagnostics and press "Copy diagnostics"; personal
  details are removed from the copy.
- Your data lives in Steam\oemt\ (config, logs, plugins, themes).

Uninstall
Close Steam, then delete Steam\wsock32.dll and the Steam\oemt folder.
